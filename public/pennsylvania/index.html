Pennsylvania Dispute Guides

🏠 Denied Homeowners Insurance Claim in Pennsylvania

Claim-handling deadlines: In Pennsylvania, your insurer must acknowledge your claim within 10 working days of receiving notice of the claim (31 Pa. Code § 146.5(a)), accept or deny it within 15 working days after receiving a properly executed proof of loss (31 Pa. Code § 146.7(a)), and pay an accepted claim promptly after acceptance once the amount is determined and undisputed. If the investigation is not complete within 30 days, the insurer must send a written delay explanation and update it every 45 days (31 Pa. Code § 146.6). Under the bad-faith statute, a court may award interest on the claim from the date it was made at the prime rate plus 3% (42 Pa.C.S. § 8371).

Bad-faith remedies: Pennsylvania recognizes statutory first-party bad faith. 42 Pa.C.S. § 8371 gives policyholders a statutory bad-faith cause of action; the Pennsylvania Supreme Court clarified the standard in Rancosky v. Washington National Ins. Co. (2017), adopting the two-part Terletsky test. A court may award interest on the claim at the prime rate plus 3%, punitive damages, and court costs and attorney's fees (42 Pa.C.S. § 8371).
